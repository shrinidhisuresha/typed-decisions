## Verdict (real-label held-out domains, 2026-10-14)

Four human-labelled domains, none in the training mixture: CLINC150, MASSIVE, GoEmotions and Measuring Hate Speech,
with 1,008–1,200 rows each. Hate speech is near-domain to Civil Comments, which is in training. At this n the CIs are about
±0.015 per task, so the gate decides. The small synthetic sets mostly could not.

- **At T=1 the adapter fails the "no fitted temperature" goal on real data.** The no-penalty `mix2` pooled result is
  Brier +0.047 (+0.032, +0.061) against post-hoc: FAIL. The stable-penalty recipe over 3 seeds is +0.028 (+0.016, +0.041):
  INCONCLUSIVE, but close to failing.
- **Accuracy transfers.** Pooled accuracy is +0.030 (no penalty) and +0.025 (penalty); both CIs exclude 0. Per task:
  MASSIVE +2 to +6 points, GoEmotions about +10 points; hate-speech levels −3 to −6 points.
- **The penalty helps calibration on real data.** It beats the no-penalty run on pooled Brier at T=1 (+0.028 vs +0.047), and wins on
  MASSIVE and GoEmotions. The small synthetic sets suggested the opposite; that was noise.
- **With a fitted temperature, the adapter is the better model on 2 of 4 domains.** Tuned and post-hoc are both cross-fitted (Brier diff, 95% CI):

**Conclusion:** the adapter is a better scorer, more accurate and with lower Brier once temperature-scaled, on real Choice and Noul data. It is
not yet calibrated without a temperature, and ordinal ratings are worse than the post-hoc pipeline in every config. The next
